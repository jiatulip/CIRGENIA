## Your first visit as owner

Open the private owner studio and sign in with the ChatGPT account that owns this site. Open Messages et activité and press Actualiser. Messages and their contact details are private. Page-view counts are estimates, not unique visitors or identities. Replies open your email application; automatic email notifications are not enabled.

The public website works independently of your Mac and Antigravity. Visitors need only the website link or QR code.

## Printing

The French and English prospectuses in public/documents now contain QR codes that open their matching website language. Standalone PNG and SVG codes are in public/qr. Keep the white margin around each QR code. Print at least 30 mm wide and scan a physical sample with your phone before printing a full batch. The codes were decoded successfully from rendered copies of both PDFs.

Keep this website address unchanged after printing. If you later use a new domain, retain the original address or a redirect so printed codes continue working.

## Editing on your Mac

Read ANTIGRAVITY_GUIDE.md and open the entire project folder in Antigravity. Run `node scripts/macbook.mjs` in that folder to start the local working version. Local test messages are separate from real online messages. Changes to local code need publication; online studio text edits are saved directly.
